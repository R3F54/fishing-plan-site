Fishing Plan AI Support

Need help, have a question, or want to report an issue? This page provides the official support contact and help links for Fishing Plan AI.

Last updated: June 6, 2026

Contact Support

For questions, feedback, bug reports, subscription help, account deletion help, or other support requests, contact us at fishingplanai@gmail.com.

We usually respond within 1-2 business days.

Subscription Help

Fishing Plan AI may offer optional auto-renewable subscriptions, including Pro and Platinum plans, through the App Store or Google Play. Subscription prices and billing periods are shown before purchase.

On iOS, you can manage or cancel a subscription from Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Fishing Plan AI. On Android, you can manage subscriptions from Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.

Restore Purchases

If you purchased a subscription but it is not active in the app, open the upgrade or subscription screen in Fishing Plan AI and use the Restore Purchases option. If the issue continues, contact support with the email address used for your store account.

Account Deletion

You can request account deletion from inside the app or follow the public instructions on our account deletion page.
