Privacy Policy

This page describes how Fishing Plan collects, uses and protects user data across the app and related services.

Effective date: May 20, 2026

1. Data We Collect

Fishing Plan collects only the data needed to generate plans and personalize the experience. This may include device identifier, preferred spot locations, catch log entries, selected tackle profile, plan date, fishing technique and distance preferences. If the user signs in, an email address or an anonymous identity provider identifier from Google or Apple may also be stored.

2. Spot Records

Spots added by the user are private by default. If a user marks a spot as public, the spot name and location may become visible on community-facing views. Private spots are not shared with third parties and are used only to support the user's own planning flow.

3. Catch Log Entries

Catch logs are also private by default. Public entries may contribute anonymously to regional fish presence statistics and community summary reporting, but other users cannot access a private individual's full raw records.

4. AI Queries

To generate plans and assistant commentary, Fishing Plan may send spot location, date, tackle profile and weather data to the AI model pipeline. These requests are processed without being tied to a user's direct personal identity and are not used for model training.

5. Advertising and Tracking

Fishing Plan uses non-personalized advertising only. No behavioral profile is created for ad delivery. Ad content may depend on the general app context and region. Ad data is not sold to third parties.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

When a user deletes their account, user-specific data stored for that account, such as plans, spots, catch logs and tackle profile information, is intended to be permanently removed from the app's managed backend systems. Public spot or catch log contributions may also be removed from community statistics where applicable.

7. User Rights

Users in Turkey may have rights under KVKK Law No. 6698, and users in Europe may have rights under GDPR, including rights to access, correct, delete and transfer personal data.

8. Third-Party Services

The app may integrate with Supabase, Google AdMob, Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In and weather-related data providers. Payments, where applicable, are handled by the App Store or Google Play.
